# OpusLists

Lists and tasks that live on your PC. One app for plain checklists and for dated tasks with real Windows alerts, in a tabbed layout.

Website: [opuslists.app](https://opuslists.app)

## What it does

1. Tabs for Today, Scheduled, Lists, All, and Done, with counts. The overdue count shows in red on Today.
2. Lists with a name, color, and icon. Items have a checkbox, notes, steps (subtasks), priority, due date, optional time, and repeat.
3. A checklist item and a dated task are the same thing, so any item can gain a due date later.
4. Native Windows toast alerts at the due time, plus an optional early alert (10 minutes, 1 hour, or 1 day).
5. Runs in the system tray. Closing the window hides it, and alerts keep firing.
6. A quick add window that works from any app, on **Ctrl+Alt+L** by default.
7. Share a list as a plain checklist (one item per line, the most dependable way into Apple Reminders), as Markdown, or as a calendar file (`.ics`) with alarms.
8. Everything is stored on your PC. No account, no server, and no sync yet.

OpusLists is part of the [OpusNexus](../README.md) family.
