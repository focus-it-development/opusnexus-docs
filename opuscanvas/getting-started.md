# Getting started with OpusCanvas

## Install

1. Download the installer from your beta invitation and run it. It installs for your account only, so no admin rights are needed.
2. Beta builds are not code signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC." Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The first time OpusCanvas opens, a short tour shows the basics. **Take the tour** on the start screen replays it.

## Capture

1. Press **Ctrl+Shift+S** from anywhere, even with OpusCanvas closed to the tray, or click **New capture**.
2. The Windows snip bar appears. Pick region, window, or full screen, then make your snip.
3. The capture opens in OpusCanvas, ready to mark up.

**Full screen** captures your main monitor without the snip bar. **Delay** (3 or 5 seconds) gives you time to open a menu before the capture.

## Mark it up

Pick a tool from the toolbar or press its key: arrow, box, ellipse, line, pen, highlighter, text, numbered step, blur, and crop. Choose a color and thickness beside the tools. Select a markup to move it, and press Delete to remove it. Undo and redo cover everything, including crop.

## Save and share

1. Captures save right away as PNG files in `Pictures\Screenshots`, the same folder Windows uses. Changes save a moment after you make them.
2. **Copy** puts the marked-up image on the clipboard, ready to paste into email, chat, or a document.
3. **Save as** puts a copy anywhere you choose.
4. Opened JPEG files are left alone, and the marked-up version saves as a new PNG.

## The tray

Closing the window keeps OpusCanvas in the system tray so Ctrl+Shift+S keeps working. Right-click the tray icon for New capture, Full screen, Open, or Quit.
