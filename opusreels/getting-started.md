# Getting started with OpusReels

## Install

1. Download the installer from your beta invitation and run it.
2. Beta builds are not code signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC." Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Make clips

1. Press **Ctrl+O** or drop a video on the window. MP4, MOV and MKV work.
2. Wait while OpusReels transcribes and analyzes the video. The time estimate depends on the length of the video and the speed of your computer.
3. Pick a suggested clip from the list. Each one says why it was picked. Trim the start and end, or add your own clip.
4. Choose a layout. For gameplay with a facecam, check the box OpusReels found around the cam and drag it if needed. Save the layout as a profile to use it on your next video.
5. Set the captions: style, font, colors, size and position.
6. Export. Files are 1080 by 1920, and each file's length is checked after export.

Press **Space** to play or pause the preview.

## Save your work

**Ctrl+S** saves a project (`.opusreels`) with your clips, layout, captions and transcript. Double-click a project to open it again. The project points to your video, so keep the video where it is.

## Speed

Export uses your graphics card when there is one (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) and falls back to the processor, which is slower. Transcription is the slowest step on older computers.
