# OpusReels

Turn a long video into short vertical clips with captions, on your own computer. A local alternative to cloud clip tools: your video is never uploaded and never changed.

Website: [opusreels.app](https://opusreels.app)

## What it does

1. **Open a video.** MP4, MOV or MKV, including phone and screen recordings with a variable frame rate.
2. **It listens.** OpusReels transcribes the speech with word timing, finds the pauses and the loud moments, and looks for a facecam, with progress and a time estimate.
3. **Pick from suggested clips.** Clips are ranked, and each one lists its reasons in plain words. There is no "virality score". Trim by hand, or add your own clip.
4. **Lay it out for vertical.** Five layouts: center crop, whole frame over a blurred copy, facecam on top with gameplay below, gameplay on top with the cam below, and facecam in a corner. Drag and resize the boxes and watch the 9:16 preview change. Save a layout as a streamer profile and reuse it.
5. **Add captions.** The spoken word is highlighted. Choose a style, any installed font, colors, size, words per line, and a position clear of the cam.
6. **Export.** Each clip is cut exactly and rendered at 1080 by 1920 in one pass, on your graphics card when there is one. SRT and VTT subtitles can be saved too.
7. **Save the project.** A `.opusreels` file keeps your clips, layout, captions and transcript. Your video is only referenced, never copied or edited.

## Free edition

Exports carry a small OpusReels mark at the top left (it moves to the top right if a face would sit under it) and a 2 second end card over the last moments of the clip. A Pro license that removes them is planned.

## Privacy

Transcription, face finding and video work all happen on your computer. The app makes no network requests. Settings and streamer profiles stay in the app data folder for your Windows user.

OpusReels is part of the [OpusNexus](../README.md) family.
