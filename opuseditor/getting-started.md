# Getting started with OpusEditor

## Install

1. Download the installer from your beta invitation and run it. It installs for your account only, so no admin rights are needed.
2. Beta builds are not code signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC." Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Open a picture

1. Click Open, press **Ctrl+O**, or drop a file on the window. PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, and HEIC photos open, along with OpusEditor project files.
2. To work on a picture that is on the clipboard, press **Ctrl+V** on the start screen.
3. HEIC photos from an iPhone open like any other picture. They are read by a small helper that loads only when you open one.

## Adjust

Open the adjustment panel and move the sliders: exposure, brightness, contrast, highlights, shadows, saturation, warmth, tint, sharpen, and blur. The picture updates as you drag. **Auto** picks a starting point, and **Compare** shows the original while you hold it.

## Crop, rotate, and resize

1. Press **C** for Crop. Draw freely or pick a fixed shape. A thirds guide helps with composition.
2. Press **Enter** to apply the crop or **Esc** to cancel it.
3. Rotate, flip, straighten, and resize are in the image panel.

These change the picture itself. Undo takes them back while the file is open.

## Mark it up

Pick a tool from the rail or press its key: arrow, line, box, oval, text, marker, numbered steps, or pen. Click an annotation to select it, then move it, resize it, rotate it, or change its color. You can also reorder, duplicate, lock, copy, and paste annotations. Press **Enter** on a selected text annotation to edit it.

## Hide sensitive parts

Press **B** for Hide, then drag over the part you want to protect. Choose blur, pixelate, or a black box. The pixels underneath are removed from the picture, so a saved file cannot give them back. Keep a project file of the unhidden original if you may need it later.

## Save and export

1. **Ctrl+S** saves a project file (`.opusedit`) with everything still editable. OpusEditor does not autosave, and it asks before closing a picture with unsaved changes.
2. **Ctrl+E** exports a picture as PNG, JPG, or WebP, with quality control for JPG and WebP. Your project stays as it is.
3. For PNG there is an **Editable PNG** option that keeps the annotations editable when the file is opened in OpusEditor.
4. **Ctrl+Shift+C** copies the finished picture to the clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

## Limits

Pictures can be up to 16384 pixels on a side and 120 megapixels.
